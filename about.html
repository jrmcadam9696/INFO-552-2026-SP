Who I Am

Hi! I'm Jason! I'm a Drexel University student specializing in Human-Computer Intraction and UX, driven by a need to understand human behavior and how interafce design can guide these behaviors for a more fluid and immersive visual and aesthetic ecperience. My work spans UX research, interface design, and front-end development weaving research insights into intuitive, accessible experiences for any and all end users.

My primary areas of focus are UX design, UX research, humans in the loop, and front-end development. I enjoy turning research insights into interfaces that are intuitive, accessible, and genuinely useful.
