Location

The Shambhala Meditation Center is located in Center City Philadelphia, on the third floor of 2030 Sansom Street.

Public Transit

Regional rail to Suburban Station in City Center is a 5 block walk to the Shambhala Center.

Parking

Metered on-street parking is found in the neighborhood for 1 to 2 hours, 7 days a week, and free after 10pm. On Chestnut Street between 30th and 23rd St on the left side going east, parking is available for up to 4 hours, 7 days a week, and free after 8pm.

The closest garage is across the street from the Center on Sansom St. There is another garage in the next block west on Sansom St. between 21–22nd St on the left.
